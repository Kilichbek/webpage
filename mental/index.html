Towards AI-Assisted Psychotherapy: Emotion-Guided Generative Interventions

Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) hold promise for therapeutic interventions, yet most existing datasets rely solely on text, overlooking non- verbal emotional cues essential to real-world therapy. To address this, we introduce a multi- modal dataset of 1,441 publicly sourced ther- apy session videos containing both dialogue and non-verbal signals such as facial expres- sions and vocal tone. Inspired by Hochschild’s concept of emotional labor, we propose a computational formulation of emotional dis- sonance—the mismatch between facial and vo- cal emotion—and use it to guide emotionally aware prompting. Our experiments show that integrating multimodal cues, especially disso- nance, improves the quality of generated inter- ventions. We also find that LLM-based evalua- tors misalign with expert assessments in this do- main, highlighting the need for human-centered evaluation.
